# All Things Retro
I create easy-to-follow guides for setting up retro gaming systems, covering everything from choosing the right hardware and controllers to installing emulators, configuring displays, and organising game libraries. My aim is to help beginners and enthusiasts build reliable, enjoyable retro gaming setups with clear instructions and practical tips. In this guide I will be setting this up on my AYN Thor which I absolutely love and has got be back into retro games I can play when lounging on the sofa. 

## Recommended Accessories (optional)
- MicroSD Card (I'd go for 128GB then scale up depending on how much out play)
- MicroSD Card Reader
- HDMI Out (If you want to extend to an external display)
- Fast Charger

## Recommended Pre-setups
- Setup a Google account
- Pre-configure your ROM's and BIOS's (test they are working beofre export)

## First things out the box
Upon opening the box to your shiny new device.
1. Connect to your home internet
2. Then head to **Settings** > **System** > **Thor OTA**
3. Check for updates
4. It may require a device restart depending on the firmware (always recommended restart after every large update)

*The current AYN Thor version of me writing this guide is version: 1.0.0.377*
